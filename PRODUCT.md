# Scholr — Product Context

## Product Name
Scholr

## Tagline
Where Schools, Teachers & Families Connect

## Register
brand + product (landing page = brand; portals = product)

## Product Purpose
Scholr is a premium mobile-first Progressive Web App that replaces fragmented school communication — email threads, WhatsApp groups, SMS blasts, paper notices — with one intelligent, beautifully designed platform used by parents, teachers, and school administrators.

## Users

### Maria — The Concerned US Parent
- 38, working mother, Houston TX, iPhone 14 Pro
- Wants real-time awareness of her child without calling the school
- Missed events, no classroom visibility, slow school apps
- Upgrade trigger: sees the Weekly Intelligence Report preview

### Mr. James — The Overworked Teacher
- 44, secondary teacher, Lagos Nigeria, Android mid-range
- Wants to communicate without using his personal phone number
- Evening messages, paper registers, report writing takes 3 full days per term
- Upgrade trigger: AI Report Writer saves him 4 hours

### Dr. Adaeze — The School Owner / Proprietor
- 52, school proprietor, Abuja + London, MacBook + iPhone, manages 2 campuses remotely
- Wants full school visibility, automated fee collection, parent satisfaction scores
- Fee defaults in Excel, no visibility into teacher-parent communication
- Upgrade trigger: School Health Score — one number that tells her everything

## Brand Tone
Premium, editorial, warm but precise. "Teachable for school management." Not enterprise SaaS. Not a government portal. Consumer-grade polish with institutional trust.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards with blue gradients
- Government/public sector UI (heavy, bureaucratic)
- Facebook/WhatsApp aesthetic (casual social media)
- Edtech toy apps (childish, playful without purpose)
- ClassDojo aesthetic (overly playful, cartoon-like)

## Strategic Principles
1. Every free feature has a deliberate ceiling that triggers upgrade at the moment of frustration
2. The Weekly Intelligence Report is the single most powerful retention and upgrade driver
3. Schools pay once — all parents, teachers, and admins get access
4. Mobile-first PWA — feels like a native app, installs from Safari/Chrome
5. AI features are named and visible, not hidden infrastructure

## Target Markets
United States, United Kingdom, Nigeria, Ghana, Canada

## Pricing
- Free: $0/mo, up to 100 students
- Pro: $89/mo or $890/yr, up to 500 students
- Enterprise: Custom, multi-campus

## Revenue Target
$1M ARR within 24 months

## Tech Stack
Next.js 14 App Router + Supabase + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion + shadcn/ui
Stripe (payments) + Claude API (AI features) + Twilio (SMS) + DeepL (translation)
