# Product Building Playbook

A complete framework for building any product, from zero to scale.
Every skill listed maps to a `/skill-name` command you can invoke directly.

**Total skills: 202 across 10 phases + 3 cross-cutting layers**

---

## How to Use This Playbook

1. **Identify your phase** — where are you right now?
2. **Pick the skills** — invoke the ones relevant to your current problem
3. **Follow the flow** — phases are sequential but you will loop back (especially 1→2→3)
4. **Cross-cutting skills** — available in any phase, always relevant

> You don't need to complete every skill in a phase before moving to the next.
> Use what the current problem demands.

---

## The 10 Phases

```
PHASE 0   →   PHASE 1   →   PHASE 2   →   PHASE 3   →   PHASE 4
Foundation     Discover      Define        Design         Build

     ↓              ↑             ↑
PHASE 5        PHASE 6   →   PHASE 7   →   PHASE 8   →   PHASE 9   →   PHASE 10
Secure &       Plan &         Measure       Market &       Launch &       Iterate &
Comply         Prioritize     & Learn       Grow           Operate        Scale
```
